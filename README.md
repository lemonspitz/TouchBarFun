# TouchBarFun! 🐾

A virtual pet, 10 sceneries and 50+ games that live on your MacBook Pro's Touch Bar, including single-player arcade games,
2-player games for two people sharing the bar, and 🥁 Drum Beat, a Taiko-style rhythm game you can load your own songs into.

Made by **[@lemonspitz](https://github.com/lemonspitz)** · version 1.0

## Install

**Requirements:** an Apple Silicon Mac with a Touch Bar (13-inch MacBook Pro M1 2020 or M2 2022), macOS 11 or later.

**One-line install (easiest):** paste this into Terminal and press Return:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lemonspitz/TouchBarFun/main/install.sh | bash
```

It downloads the latest release into Applications and opens it. Look for 🐾 in the menu bar and on the Touch Bar.

**Or download the DMG:** get [TouchBarFun.dmg](https://github.com/lemonspitz/TouchBarFun/releases/latest/download/TouchBarFun.dmg),
open it, and drag **TouchBarFun!** onto **Applications**. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS
will say it can't verify it. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**.
You only need to do this once.

To start it automatically, choose 🐾 → **Open at Login**.

## Features

- **Touch Bar**: a 🐾 button sits in the Control Strip; tap it to show the pet system-wide.
  The bar has the animal picker, 🍖 feed, 🎾 ball, 💤 sleep, 🎮 games, 🏞 scenery, and the pet scene, which stretches to fill the free space.
  The animal, 🎮 and 🏞 buttons swap the whole bar for a menu showing every choice at once (current one highlighted);
  picking one returns to the main bar, and ◀︎ goes back without changing anything.
- **Menu bar** 🐾: show on Touch Bar, pet window (mouse-playable), animal, scenery, games, rename, sound effects, open at login.
- **19 animals**, each with a favourite food: Puppy, Kitty, Bunny, Fox, Penguin, Panda, Froggy, Dino, Piggy, Bear, Koala,
  Unicorn, Owl, Turtle, Hamster, Lion, Chick, Dragon, Elephant.
- **10 sceneries** with day/night versions, scrolling backgrounds, ground decorations and ambient effects:
  🌼 Meadow (butterflies, fireflies), 🏖 Beach (seagulls), ❄️ Snowy Peaks (snowfall), 🏜 Desert (shooting stars),
  🌲 Forest (falling leaves, fireflies), 🏙 City (lit windows, street lamps), 🌸 Sakura Garden (petals),
  🚀 Space (planets, shooting stars), 🐠 Under the Sea (bubbles, fish, light rays), 🍭 Candy Land (sprinkles).
- **37 single-player games** (🎮 on the Touch Bar; tap “More” to page through):
  - 🏃 Hop Runner: tap to jump cacti
  - 🧺 Treat Catch: slide to catch treats, dodge rocks
  - 🫧 Bubble Pop: tap bubbles, chain combos
  - ⚾ Home Run Derby: time your swing; 3 strikes and you're out
  - 🧤 Play Catch: catch your pet's throws, then throw back (farther = more points)
  - 🏁 Run Race: 100 m dash vs 3 rivals; tap fast, alternating fingers
  - 🎣 Fishing: cast, then tap the instant the fish bites
  - 🧠 Copy Cat: repeat your pet's pattern on 4 coloured pads
  - 🎈 Balloon Ride: hold to rise, let go to sink; dodge birds, trees and storm clouds
  - ⚽ Keepy Uppy: tap beside the ball when it's low to keep it in the air
  - 🔨 Whack-a-Mole: bonk moles (gold = bonus), never your friend
  - 🍉 Fruit Slice: swipe across fruit, avoid bombs
  - 🏹 Archery: hold to draw, release at the right power; mind the wind
  - 🥁 Drum Beat: pick a song and difficulty (slide left/right to scroll the song list, tap to pick), then drum along anywhere right of the target — 🔴 1 finger, 🔵 2 fingers,
    big 🟠 3 fingers, big 🟣 4 fingers (pet window: click / right-click, add Shift for the big ones). Big notes sit on the
    song's heaviest moments and score double; hit notes stop in the target and pop there, and “early / late” shows your timing. ✕ is on the far left. The notes are generated from the song's melody: red on strong beats and low notes,
    blue on weak beats, off-beats and high notes; Hard also drums the accompaniment. Graded S–D, best score kept per song.
  - 🌉 Bridge Builder: hold to stretch a plank, release when it reaches the next ledge
  - 🧱 Tower Stack: tap to drop sliding blocks; overhangs are cut off, perfect drops widen the tower
  - 🚗 Highway Dash: tap to switch lanes, dodge traffic, grab coins
  - 🍳 Pancake Chef: flip when golden, tap again to serve; don't let them burn
  - 🃏 Pair Match: find the 8 matching animal pairs in as few moves as possible
  - ➕ Math Dash: tap the right answer; it gets harder as you go
  - 👾 Alien Attack: slide your ship, it fires by itself; dodge bombs, clear waves
  - 🏀 Hoops: drag to pull back, release to shoot; swishes score 3
  - 🔤 Word Scramble: tap the letters in order to spell the word
  - 🏓 Ping Pong: tap as the ball reaches your paddle; rally against your pet
  - 🎳 Bowling: lock your aim, then your power; 5 frames
  - 🐸 Road Crossing: tap to hop lane by lane between the cars; wait on the grass strips
  - 🧸 Claw Machine: tap to drop the claw; gems are worth more but harder to grab
  - 🎆 Fireworks: tap rockets right at the top of their climb
  - ⛳ Mini Golf: drag for power, release to putt over the hills; 6 holes
  - 🎰 Lucky Slots: tap to spin, then tap to stop each reel (7️⃣7️⃣7️⃣ = jackpot)
  - 💣 Mine Row: minesweeper in one row; tap to reveal, hold (or right-click) to flag
  - 🎨 Color Clash: tap the INK color, not the word (watch for “WORD” rounds)
  - ⏱ Perfect Timing: stop the clock on target after it hides; 5 rounds
  - 🔦 Hide & Seek: slide the flashlight, lift your finger on your pet (not the decoys)
  - 🏎 Drag Race: shift when the needle hits green; beat the rival car
  - ⛷️ Ski Jump: tap at the edge, hold to glide, let go before landing; 3 jumps
  - 🏙 Missile Defense: fire laser beams to protect your cities, wave after wave
- **19 two-player games** (🎮 → “2 Players ▶︎”, marked 2P). Player 1 🔴 uses the left half of the Touch Bar, Player 2 🔵 the right half,
  at the same time. Each starts with a choice: tap the left side for 👤 1 Player against a computer rival animal, or the right side for 👥 2 Players.
  - 🤠 Quick Draw: tap only on DRAW! (watch out for fake words), first to 3
  - 🪢 Tug of War: mash your side to pull the rope, best of 3
  - 🚧 Hurdle Race: two lanes; tap your side to jump your runner's hurdles
  - 🎴 Snap!: tap when the card matches the one before; wrong snaps cost a point
  - 💥 Pop Battle: mirrored bubbles on each side, most pops in 30 s wins
  - 🥅 Soccer: your animal follows your finger; run into the ball to kick, first to 5
  - ✊ Rock Paper Scissors: pick a hand on your side before SHOOT!, first to 3
  - 🧮 Math Battle: first to tap the right answer scores; wrong answers freeze you briefly
  - 💣 Hot Potato: throw the bomb back when “!” appears; holding it at BOOM loses
  - 🛸 Space Duel: tap to fire at the bobbing ship, first to 5 hits
  - 🎾 Tennis Duel: tap as the ball reaches your paddle, first to 5; every return speeds the ball up
  - ⭕ Tic-Tac-Toe: take turns (the 3 board rows are laid side by side), first to 3
  - 🔴 Four in a Row: take turns dropping discs, line up four
  - 🥊 Boxing: tap to jab, hold to block; blocked jabs leave you dizzy, first to 7
  - 💥 Cannon Duel: take turns; hold for power, release to fire, mind the wind
  - 🎈 Pump It: pump the biggest balloon in 8 s without popping it, first to 3
  - 🧩 Memory Duel: both repeat the growing pattern on your own pads; last one standing wins
  - 🐎 Gallop Race: tap on the beat to gallop; off-beat taps make you stumble
  - 🔢 Number Rush: tap 1 → 8 in order on your side; wrong taps freeze you
  Scores earn ⭐ and XP, and the pet grows with its level.
- **Drum Beat songs** (played through macOS's built-in General MIDI instruments):
  - 🎻 Public-domain classics: Ode to Joy, Twinkle Variations (Mozart), Für Elise, Eine kleine Nachtmusik, Turkish March,
    Minuet in G, Canon in D, Hall of the Mountain King, Brahms' Lullaby, Sakura Sakura, Jingle Bells
  - ✨ Originals written for the app in a J-pop style: Spring Light, Night Train, Blue Hour, Glow Stick
  - 📁 Your own songs: 🐾 menu → “Import Song for Drum Beat (MIDI or audio)…” (copied to
    ~/Library/Application Support/TouchBarFun/Songs).
    - MIDI files: the full arrangement plays and the drum chart follows its melody part.
    - 🎧 Audio files (MP3, M4A, WAV…): choose the section to play; the recording is analysed for its tempo and hits,
      and the notes land on them (bass-heavy hits red, bright snare/hat/clap hits blue). Your file just plays from disk.
- Pet needs (fullness, happiness, energy) persist between launches and day/night follows the clock.

## Build from source

Needs only the Xcode Command Line Tools (`xcode-select --install`).

- `./build.sh` builds `build/TouchBarFun!.app` (arm64, ad-hoc signed).
- `./release.sh` also packages `build/release/TouchBarFun.dmg` and `TouchBarFun.zip` for a GitHub release.

Testing helpers:
- `build/obj/TouchBarFun-arm64 --preview <dir>` renders PNG previews of every animal and game.
- `build/obj/TouchBarFun-arm64 --selftest` plays every newer game with a bot and prints the results.
- `open "build/TouchBarFun!.app" --args --start race` launches straight into a game.
- `open "build/TouchBarFun!.app" --args --menu games` opens a Touch Bar menu (`animals`, `games` or `scenery`).
